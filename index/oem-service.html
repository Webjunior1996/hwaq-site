Custom Packaging Made Simple

From design and sampling to production and delivery, we provide full-process support for custom eco-friendly packaging solutions for food service and retail customers.

  • 1. Requirement Analysis Understand your product type, market needs, size, material, and packaging requirements.
  • 2. Design & Development Support structure design, material selection, printing layout, and functional packaging development.
  • 3. Sampling & Adjustment Provide samples for testing, review, and improvement before mass production.
  • Testing & Compliance Check product performance, food safety, and relevant compliance requirements.
  • 5. Production & Delivery Arrange mass production, quality inspection, packaging, and delivery after sample approval.
  • Tailored Packaging Solutions
    We provide tailored packaging solutions through in-house design and engineering support. Our team works closely with customers to develop products that match brand identity, structural requirements, and practical application needs.
  • Material & Process Innovation
    We continuously improve materials and production processes to develop more practical and eco-friendly packaging solutions. Our work focuses on biodegradable coatings, recyclable structures, and lightweight designs, helping improve product performance while supporting environmental responsibility.
  • Performance Testing & Compliance Support
    Our products are tested for food safety, leak resistance, heat resistance, and environmental requirements. With strict quality checks and certification support, we help customers meet market-specific compliance needs.
  • Professional Sales Team
    With over 10 years of export experience, our sales team supports customers in English, Spanish, Russian, and other languages. We provide timely communication, product guidance, and customized solutions to help meet different market and business needs.
  • Efficient Logistics Support
    Located in the Yangtze River Delta region, we benefit from convenient transportation and mature logistics resources. We support sea, air, and land transportation options, with order tracking and delivery coordination to help customers receive goods safely and efficiently.
  • Reliable Material Sourcing
    Our purchasing team works closely with qualified raw material suppliers to maintain a stable and traceable supply of eco-friendly materials. Through careful supplier selection and cost management, we help support consistent product quality, efficient production, and flexible order fulfillment.
