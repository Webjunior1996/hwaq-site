Anhui Guanxuan Focused on Eco-Friendly Packaging Solutions

Anhui Guanxuan Environmental Protection Technology Co., Ltd. was established in March 2016 and is located in the Yangtze River Delta region, with convenient transportation and efficient logistics support.

The company specializes in the research, development, production, and sales of eco-friendly packaging products for food service and retail applications. With over 200 units of production equipment and multiple production lines for lamination, printing, paper cups, paper bowls, plastic products, and tableware packaging, we provide stable manufacturing capacity and flexible customization services for customers.
